When Growth Outpaces Your Systems

Growing businesses don't usually have a technology problem. They have a systems problem.

As businesses grow, the tools, processes, and workarounds that once worked start creating friction. Information gets scattered. Manual work multiplies. Teams build their own processes. And the technology meant to make work easier often creates more complexity.

Common signs your systems aren't keeping up
01

Disconnected Tools

Systems don't share the information they should.

02

Manual Work

Repetitive tasks consume time that could be spent elsewhere.

03

Scattered Data

Important information lives across platforms, spreadsheets, and inboxes.

04

Inconsistent Processes

Work depends on who is doing it instead of a system everyone can follow.

05

Limited Visibility

Leaders struggle to see what's working, what's not, and where growth is coming from.

Built Differently

Systems should work for people. Not the other way around.

The best system isn't the one with the most features. It's the one people can actually use—and that makes the business behind them work better.

At Build & Measure, we design with both sides of the business in mind: the people doing the work and the leaders responsible for understanding it. That means fewer unnecessary steps for users, better data for decision-makers, less room for error, and systems that solve real problems instead of creating new ones.

How we think about every system we build

01

Meet people where they are

We design for the people who will actually use the system—not an idealized version of how they should work. Every decision considers usability, adoption, and the realities of the team.

02

Solve for both sides

A better employee experience and better business visibility don't have to compete. We look for solutions that reduce work for users while improving consistency, automation, data, and reporting for leadership.

03

Listen from every angle

The best solution rarely comes from one perspective. We listen to the people doing the work, the people managing it, and the data behind it to understand what actually needs to change.

04

Look beyond the obvious problem

We don't just fix the symptom in front of us. We look for opportunities to solve multiple points of friction with a smarter, more connected solution.

And when we think we're finished, we ask one more question:

“What's one way we can make this even better?”

Experience Behind the Work

Built from experience. Not theory.

Build & Measure is new. The work behind it isn't.

Our founders bring hands-on experience designing and building operational systems inside growing organizations—from CRM architecture and automation to custom integrations, attribution, reporting, and the processes that connect them.

01

Marketing Attribution & Systems Integration

Connecting marketing spend to actual business outcomes.

A fragmented advertising and CRM ecosystem made it difficult to connect marketing investment with the leads and clients it actually produced.

Ad Spend → Lead → HubSpot → Client → Conversion Feedback → Reporting
The Build

A custom attribution ecosystem connecting HubSpot with Google Ads, Google LSAs, and Meta—tracking lifecycle progression inside the CRM and returning conversion outcomes to advertising platforms.

The Impact

Leadership gained clearer visibility into spend, leads, clients, conversion performance, and customer acquisition cost—creating better information for advertising and budget decisions.

02

Event Operations & Measurement

Turning seminars into a measurable growth channel.

Seminar activity existed across multiple stages, making it difficult to connect registrations and attendance with the consultations, deals, and revenue that followed.

Seminar → Registration → Attendance → Consultation → Deal → Revenue
The Build

A CRM-based tracking architecture using custom objects, unique registration codes, association labels, custom properties, workflows, and coded automation.

The Impact

The organization gained a clearer view of event performance and a foundation for measuring conversion rates, venue performance, return on investment, and post-seminar revenue.

03

CRM & Operational Infrastructure

Building operations into the systems people already use.

Critical processes across intake, sales, internal requests, and client feedback depended on manual follow-up and disconnected tools.

Request → Route → Act → Validate → Measure
The Build

CRM systems for pipeline controls, task creation, ownership and routing, internal ticketing, escalations, client surveys, review campaigns, notifications, and cross-platform data movement.

The Impact

More operational work could happen inside connected systems, with clearer ownership, fewer manual follow-ups, better visibility, and stronger data for the people managing the business.
